Frequently asked questions

A few things you might want to know.

How much does a website cost?

The cost depends on the number of pages, features and content required. Contact us with your needs and we'll provide a quote before work begins.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Websites are planned with responsive layouts so visitors can use them on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

What information do you need from me?

Usually your business name, logo (if available), services, prices, contact details, location and photos or other content you'd like included.

Can you update my website later?

Yes. Website updates and ongoing maintenance can be discussed as a separate service.

Do I need a domain name?

A custom domain gives your website its own web address. Domain registration and hosting costs are discussed separately and should be agreed before purchase.

How do I get started?

Send a WhatsApp message or email with a short description of your business and the website you're looking for.
